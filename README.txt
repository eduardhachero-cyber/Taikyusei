TAIKYUSEI WEBSITE
=================

This is a responsive online product catalog with:
- Product selection
- Add-to-cart and quantity controls
- Cart saved in the browser
- Checkout/order-request form
- SEO metadata
- Product/website structured data
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml
- Mobile-friendly design

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in index.html, robots.txt and sitemap.xml with your real domain.
2. Replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com in index.html with the official order email.
3. Add your official phone, Messenger/WhatsApp link, address, delivery rules and payment instructions.
4. Upload the entire folder to your hosting provider.
5. Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit sitemap.xml.
6. Google indexing is not guaranteed or immediate; Search Console helps Google discover the site.
